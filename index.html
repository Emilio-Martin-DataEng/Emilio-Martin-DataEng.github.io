How we prove it

A dashboard is only useful once you know it is right. Before handover, your owner and your accountant agree about twenty questions and their correct answers. Then we ask Claude, and you get the scorecard.

On the Wide World Importers sample, Claude Dashboards answered 20 out of 20 correctly, straight off the raw sales tables. Where a question could be read two ways, it said which reading it used.

The product inside: FactSmith Ask

Ask is the governed answer layer we bring to every engagement. It knows what your business means by revenue, customer and on time, and it enforces who may see what beneath the AI, not by it.

It runs on your own network for teams that cannot send data to a cloud AI, and it sits under Claude dashboards for teams that can.
