Trusted numbers for Claude

Every AI dashboard writes its own query. Trusted numbers puts one agreed meaning of revenue, margin and customer underneath all of them, and proves it every month.

Book a 30-minute call

What it adds

Agreed definitions

Revenue, margin, active customer, fiscal year: agreed once with the people who sign the numbers, and used by every dashboard and every person.

Access beneath the AI

Permissions are enforced at the row and the column before the model is involved. A regional manager sees their region; the rest never reaches the AI.

A monthly scorecard

Your golden questions re-run every month, with the result in your inbox. A broken load or a drifting definition shows up as a mismatch, not as a wrong decision.

Two ways to run it

On your own network. FactSmith Ask runs standalone, next to your warehouse, for teams that cannot send data to a cloud AI. Available now.

Under Claude. A FactSmith gateway gives Claude only modelled, permitted, aggregated facts, never the raw tables. In development; ask us where it stands.

The layer it is built on

Trusted numbers is FactSmith Ask, our own product: a governed layer of business meaning over a dimensional warehouse. See What is Ask, how answers are verified and trust and security.
