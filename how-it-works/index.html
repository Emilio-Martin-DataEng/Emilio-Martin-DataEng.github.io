Four steps

Connect, agree meaning, ask, keep

There is no modelling project and no BI rollout. The work is mostly agreeing what your terms mean, once.

1. Connect your data

Point FactSmith at your warehouse. It reports on dimensional models, and says so plainly if a schema is not one. The first questions are proposed and checked, not left as a blank page.

2. Agree what things mean

Define revenue, customer and on time once, so every answer uses the same definition.

3. Ask

Ask in your own words, by voice or by typing. The two are the same box.

4. Keep what matters

Keep the answers worth keeping. Have an administrator vouch for the ones that must be right.

Connecting a warehouse is guided

The first session is not a blank page.

FactSmith looks at which parts of your warehouse have never been asked about, proposes questions in ordinary business language, and checks each one actually runs and comes back with rows. An administrator then accepts or rejects them one at a time, with the query shown. Only the accepted ones count when we score whether the product is getting the numbers right.

Ask it how you would say it

A question in plain language is enough. FactSmith returns an answer as a tile you can read, keep, and ask about again.
