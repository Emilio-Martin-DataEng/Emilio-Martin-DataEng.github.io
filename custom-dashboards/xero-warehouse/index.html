    What to know before relying on it

    These are illustrative figures. They were generated to add up to the real September 2026 totals from Xero's Demo Company (UK): income £17,697.71, net profit £6,752.45, receivables £20,441.38, payables £9,079.59 and cash £5,274.30. Everything else is invented. The cash bridge adds drawings and tax as a modelled outflow and a small VAT and timing line so cash ties to the bank.

    Claude's Xero connector cannot reach most of this. Payments, budgets, items, bank transactions, tracking categories, payroll, projects and assets need data copied out through the Xero API. The explainer page lists what the connector can and cannot read.

    Xero API tiers. The Starter tier is free for up to five connections and Core covers up to fifty. The full general ledger (Journals) sits behind the Advanced tier, so for most clients we rebuild it from reports, documents and bank transactions. We are still confirming that every endpoint is open on Starter and Core, and that Payroll, Projects and Assets access can be granted.
