How the connector dashboard works

Two read-only Xero calls, Get aged receivables and Get aged payables, run on each open. Totals and the overdue split are computed on the page from the invoice and bill rows, and a Sources panel shows those rows beside Xero's own totals.

Each viewer reads through their own Xero connection, which suits an owner and their accountant.
