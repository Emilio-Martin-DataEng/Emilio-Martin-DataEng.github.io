    Can the public check this?

    We looked for the records on the stakeholders' own websites on 15 October 2026. "Not found" means not found by us, not that a record does not exist. Still, when the picture has to be assembled from press reports, that is a finding about public communication in its own right.

    In fairness. The department does publish dated statements, and its archive is a browsable, dated list. SANRAL's release is dated, and cites its gazette numbers. What is missing is project-level information: the contract, the programme, progress against it, payments, and notice of closures. In our search, the March 2026 closure and reopening plan reached the public through the press; we found no department statement on either.
